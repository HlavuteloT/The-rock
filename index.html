Planning Knowledge

Practical information to help property owners and developers better understand the planning process.

What is Land Use Management?

Land use management regulates how land may be used and developed within the applicable planning framework.

Do I Need a Planning Application?

The requirement depends on the existing zoning, proposed land use, development parameters and applicable municipal legislation.

Why Conduct a Feasibility Assessment?

Early planning assessment can help identify opportunities, restrictions and potential approval requirements before significant development costs are incurred.
